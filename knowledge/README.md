# Knowledge base for the MeVArc 2026 presentation

This directory separates evidence extraction from presentation authoring. No
Beamer source is generated or modified by this knowledge pass.

## Evidence labels

- **Article-supported** — stated or shown in `cond26/main.tex`, its generated
  result tables, or the submitted manuscript PDF.
- **Contextual source** — stated in the interim report, work plan, RFX project
  notes, correspondence summaries, or presentation-status material. These are
  useful for future directions but are not evidence from the copper article.
- **Suggested interpretation** — a proposed framing for the conference talk;
  it must not be presented as an article result without explicit approval.

## Files

- `source_inventory.md` — source files, figures, and supporting material.
- `motivation_background.md` — scientific problem and necessary background.
- `questions_methods_assumptions.md` — questions, design, methods, and assumptions.
- `results_conclusions.md` — principal findings and conclusions.
- `equations_figures_numbers_citations.md` — presentation-ready evidence index.
- `limitations_future.md` — limitations, unresolved issues, and future work.
- `confirmation_needed.md` — claims and choices requiring Yinon's confirmation.
- `style_inventory.md` — reusable presentation style material in other projects.

The proposed talk structure is in `../presentation_map.md`.
